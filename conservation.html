Striped Bass Conservation & Direct Support

A single 40-pound female striped bass carries over 3 million eggs. How we handle overslot fish on the sand determines the future of our fishery.

🌊 4 Non-Negotiable Surf Release Rules for Overslot Fish

1. Keep Them in the Wash Never drag a big breeder across dry sand—sand strips their protective slime coat and clogs gills. Unhook in ankle-deep water.
2. Support the Belly Horizontally Never hang a heavy bass vertically by the jaw or Bogagrip—their internal organs aren't built for gravity out of water.
3. Crush Barbs & Swap to Singles Replace belly trebles on SP Minnows and swimmers with inline single hooks. Safer for the fish and safer for your hands at night.
4. Mandatory Inline Circle Hooks When fishing clam or bunker chunks, NJ law requires non-offset inline circle hooks to prevent gut-hooking.
