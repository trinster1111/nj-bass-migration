28" to <31" Slot Harvest

How to Bleed & Cook Slot Bass

1. Bleed Immediately in the Wash Cut the gill rakers as soon as you measure a legal slot fish. Let it bleed out in the surf or bucket for 5 minutes before icing. Makes the fillets snow-white.
2. Remove the Red Meat (Bloodline) After skinning, trim out the dark lateral strip before cooking—that's where 95% of any "fishy" taste hides.
3. Cast-Iron Blackened or Pan-Seared Pat bone-dry, coat in Cajun seasoning, sear in ripping hot butter & avocado oil for 3 mins per side. Save the collars for the grill!
Protect the Breeder Stock

Conservation & Direct Action

Big overslot females (35"+) carry millions of eggs. Crush your barbs, swap treble hooks for inline singles, and keep big fish in the water during release.
